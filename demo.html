Mneme — Demo Planning Page

Memory made social. Feminine, archival-modern reading: warm parchment UI, deep reader controls, contextual AI, and Reading Halls where readers choose a seat and read in quiet presence together.

Reader Focus

Reader Experience

  • Typography, spacing, and margin settings
  • Light / sepia / dark themes
  • Highlights, bookmarks, and AI slide-up panel

Reading Hall Demo Interaction

  • Step 1: Enter Hall (Stoa / Scriptorium / Lyceum)
  • Step 2: Tap a free seat from the map
  • Step 3: Presence appears to nearby readers
  • Step 4: Open book and read with ambient social focus
  • Step 5: Leave seat or auto-release on idle timeout

Signature experience: social focus without noise

Architecture Plan

  • Frontend app (React + TypeScript + Hall canvas)
  • API layer with domain modules
  • PostgreSQL + Redis seat locks + object storage
  • AI orchestration with safety filters
  • Realtime presence gateway, audit logging, and alerting

Reference: ARCHITECTURE.md · AUDIT.md · RAG ADR

Security and Privacy Plan

  • Role and ownership-based access controls
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Consent-driven AI processing controls
  • Data export/deletion workflows
  • Incident response and compliance checklist

Reference: SECURITY_PRIVACY.md
